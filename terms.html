  1. Program description

    Micron Signal sends recurring SMS alerts with daily MU (Micron Technology) research results — signal direction, regime score, and trade verdicts — to a phone number the account holder has opted in.

  3. Message frequency

    Message frequency varies based on market conditions and the daily research schedule.

    Reply STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, REVOKE, or END at any time to stop receiving messages. You may receive one confirmation message after opting out. Reply START, YES, or UNSTOP to resubscribe.

    Reply HELP, INFO, or SUPPORT for assistance, or contact the account holder through the Micron Signal project.

  7. Acceptable use

    You agree not to misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, or provide a phone number you do not control or have permission to enroll.
