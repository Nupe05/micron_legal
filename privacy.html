Micron Signal · SMS Program Effective August 21, 2026

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Micron Signal collects when you opt in to SMS alerts about MU (Micron Technology) options research, how that information is used, and how to stop receiving messages.

Terms & Conditions ↗ Opt In to Alerts ↗ Track Record ↗
No mobile opt-in data is shared. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent collected through this program are never shared with, sold to, or rented to any third party or affiliate, for marketing purposes or otherwise.

  1. Who operates this program

    Micron Signal is a personal market-research and SMS alert tool operated by the account holder. It monitors MU options signals — price trend, regime score, and volatility context — and sends the resulting research and trade verdicts by text message to a phone number the account holder configures.

  2. Information we collect

    • The mobile phone number you provide when opting in to SMS alerts.
    • Opt-in and opt-out status, and the date/time consent was given or revoked.
    • Basic message delivery records (sent, delivered, failed) needed to operate the program.

    We do not collect any other personal information through this program.

  3. How we use your information

    Your phone number is used only to deliver Micron Signal alerts (regime score, signal direction, and trade verdicts), opt-in confirmations, replies to HELP, and confirmations after you reply STOP. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

  4. Sharing your information

    We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. This applies to every category of information this program collects — text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for any purpose.

  5. Message frequency and fees

    Message frequency varies based on the daily research schedule and market conditions. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

  6. Data retention

    Your phone number and opt-in status are kept only as long as needed to operate the program. When you opt out, we retain the minimum record required to honor that opt-out and prevent re-subscription by mistake.

  7. Your choices

    • Reply STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, REVOKE, or END at any time to opt out.
    • Reply HELP, INFO, or SUPPORT for assistance.
    • Reply START, YES, or UNSTOP to opt back in after stopping.

  8. Contact

    Questions about this policy can be directed to the account holder through the Micron Signal project.

  9. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as the program changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
